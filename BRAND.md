# CloudHub Signal Gateway Brand

## Product

- **Product Name:** CloudHub Signal Gateway
- **Short Name:** Signal Gateway
- **Tagline:** Turn Events Into Action.
- **Promise:** Translate opaque infrastructure events into clear, useful notifications.

## Visual Identity

The gateway arch represents a controlled boundary. The cyan pulse represents incoming event data, while the amber dot marks the useful notification produced by the rules engine.

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Foundation | Deep Navy | `#10243E` |
| Primary Action | Cloud Blue | `#1473E6` |
| Signal/Data | Signal Cyan | `#21C7D9` |
| Alert/Outcome | Alert Amber | `#F5A623` |
| Negative Space | White | `#FFFFFF` |

Use the full lockup in product headers and documentation. Preserve clear space equal to the amber notification dot around the logo. Do not recolour individual elements, distort the lockup, or place it on a low-contrast background.

## Voice

Clear, calm, operational, and specific. Prefer “Rule Matched” and “Message Sent” over vague success language. Errors should say what failed and what the operator can do next.
