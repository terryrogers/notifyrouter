# NotifyRouter Brand

## Product

- **Product Name:** NotifyRouter
- **Short Name:** NotifyRouter
- **Tagline:** Universal Notification Gateway.
- **Promise:** Turn arbitrary events into clear notifications and route them reliably to the right destination.

## Visual Identity

The split-route mark represents one incoming event being directed to multiple notification channels. The bell and envelope identify push and email delivery without tying the product to a particular provider.

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Foundation | Deep Navy | `#10243E` |
| Primary Action | Cloud Blue | `#1473E6` |
| Signal/Data | Signal Cyan | `#21C7D9` |
| Notification Accent | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Negative Space | White | `#FFFFFF` |

Use the full lockup in product headers and documentation. Preserve generous clear space around the routing mark. Do not recolour individual elements, distort the lockup, or place it on a low-contrast background.

## Voice

Clear, calm, operational, and specific. Prefer “Rule Matched” and “Message Sent” over vague success language. Errors should say what failed and what the operator can do next.
